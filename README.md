2. Magic Media — Image Generation
Generation Prompt

Create a clean, professional campaign visual for a fictional airport organisation called AeroVista Airports. Show diverse adult domestic passengers preparing for travel inside a modern international-style airport terminal. Some passengers are checking a generic digital flight-information display while others are keeping their travel documents accessible. Use a dark-blue, sky-blue and warm-yellow visual palette with bright natural lighting. Create a realistic commercial-photography style with natural human expressions and proportions. Keep the airport environment plausible and uncluttered. Do not include real airline or airport logos, airport names, readable personal information, baggage limits, security rules, fake statistics, promotional claims, or readable operational flight information. Do not portray the scene as a photograph of a specific real event.

15. Student Reflection
1. What is the purpose of a brand guide?

A brand guide helps maintain a consistent identity across different designs by defining colours, fonts, visual style and tone.

2. How did Magic Media support the campaign?

Magic Media helped create a realistic airport scene showing passengers preparing for travel and checking flight information.

3. What problems appeared after expanding the image?

The expanded design needed to be checked for passenger appearance, baggage, airport architecture, lighting and unwanted objects.

4. What element was changed using Magic Edit?

A suitcase was selected and replaced with a more realistic modern carry-on while preserving the surrounding scene.

5. Why must AI-generated image text be checked?

AI-generated text can look realistic while containing incorrect or meaningless information. In aviation, viewers could misunderstand it as genuine flight, airport or safety information.

6. Why should baggage and security rules come from official sources?

Baggage and security requirements can vary by airline, airport and journey. Official sources reduce the risk of communicating incorrect requirements.

7. How was consistency maintained across formats?

Consistency was maintained through the same campaign name, colour palette, typography, airport imagery and professional tone.

8. What changes were required after resizing?

The layout was adapted to the wider 16:9 format so the presentation title remained readable and the airport image had sufficient space.

9. How did peer review improve the campaign?

Peer review is pending. Feedback can be used to identify readability, consistency, accuracy and composition issues.

10. Why must AI-assisted designs be reviewed before publication?

AI can generate visual or textual errors even when a design looks professional. Review helps identify inaccurate information, unrealistic objects, privacy issues and misleading claims.
